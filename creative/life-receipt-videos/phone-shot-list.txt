LIFE RECEIPT • Two 14-second phone cuts

Film vertically. The MP4s already carry the story as on-screen text. To make a more personal cut, record one conversational voice memo per video and use the following beats. Leave the last three seconds quiet so people can scan the QR code.

01  Why did you frame a receipt?
0–3   Open on the whole design. Say “Why did you frame a receipt?” Pause.
3–6   Cut to the name. “It’s Sam’s fortieth. Look what’s on it.”
6–10  Cut in on the breaths line. “Nearly three hundred million breaths. Apparently the subscription’s free.”
10–14 Hold on the stamp and QR code. “Make one for your person.”
Caption idea — The one receipt I’d keep. What would yours say?

02  This birthday card got unusually long.
0–3   Show the long Sunshine design. “I may have gone overboard on Sam’s birthday card.”
3–6   Show a screen recording of yourself entering SAM in the receipt editor. “Started with a name.”
6–10  Cut to the daydreams line. “Ended up with forty-four thousand nine hundred and twenty-five daydreams. Director’s cut.”
10–14 Hold on the QR code. Let the punchline breathe.
Caption idea — A birthday gift for someone with a very active imagination.

Use the included artwork preview for any shot you haven’t filmed. If you already have the real print, shoot it flat in window light, then pull back from the name to show its length. A frame is optional styling. For phone screen recordings use fictional details and hide notifications.
