# Memento Mori receipt humour — contemporary formats and original drafts

Research date: 25 November 2026. Scope: a targeted scan of official creator, publisher, museum, product and service sources. This is not an exhaustive inventory of internet jokes. No joke-collection scraping; no protected routines reproduced. All receipt lines below are original drafting for NO REFUNDS, not quotations or claims of unprecedented wording.

## 1. Which formats make mortality funny without repeating a countdown?

### Takeaway

The strongest fit is an everyday administrative system that cannot cope with finality: warranties, accounts, customer service, unfinished messages, accumulated possessions. Alternate these mechanisms instead of listing the same remaining weeks under three names. A physical receipt supplies the setup; each value should add a new reversal.

### Cited Findings

1. **Death as employment:** the official *Mort* synopsis puts Death into an employer/apprentice relationship with ordinary workplace concerns. Transferable mechanism: the cosmic treated as an understaffed business. Do not reuse its character voice or dialogue. [Official publisher/author page](https://www.terrypratchettbooks.com/books/mort/)
2. **Afterlife as a practical communication problem:** Rob Wilkins's 2025 remembrance of Terry Pratchett connects bereavement with an everyday communication expectation, then returns to books as legacy. Transferable mechanism: a small mundane obstacle punctures a huge metaphysical question. Its actual punchline should not be reused. [Official remembrance](https://terrypratchett.com/news/remembering-sir-terry-pratchett-10-years-on/)
3. **A real final-instructions story:** Pratchett's official biography records his request to destroy unfinished writing and the use of a steamroller. Transferable mechanism: absurdly physical completion of an administrative/data-deletion task. [Official biography](https://terrypratchett.com/terry-pratchett/)
4. **Customer-service denial:** the official Monty Python channel publishes *Dead Parrot*. It is a relevant comparator for the consumer-complaint frame. Do not borrow its recognizable euphemisms, dialogue or escalating catalogue. The indexed official video description was available; the direct video fetch exposed only a shell. [Official sketch upload](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TaFDzTzKAT0)
5. **Blunt language plus paperwork:** Chanel Reynolds's checklist combines informal tone with preparing documents, finances and digital-account information. Transferable mechanism: serious affairs made approachable through a candid, everyday voice. This is inspiration for tone, not legal guidance. [Get Your Shit Together](https://getyourshittogether.org/checklist/)
6. **Death literacy through literal questions:** Caitlin Doughty's podcast page includes a book preview dealing with unusual questions about pets, bodies and cremation. Transferable mechanism: the ordinary household question taken literally, followed by a correction. Avoid turning bodily myths into asserted facts on a poster. [Order of the Good Death podcast](https://www.orderofthegooddeath.com/podcast/)
7. **Death administration is real:** the same organisation's planning guide emphasizes preparing documents, appointing trusted people and recording wishes. Transferable mechanism: even the last event generates a handover. [End-of-life planning](https://www.orderofthegooddeath.com/resources/end-of-life-planning/)
8. **Tea, cake and mortality:** Death Cafe describes conversations about finite lives alongside tea and cake, in a respectful setting. Transferable mechanism: domestic hospitality lowers the temperature of the subject. [Death Cafe](https://deathcafe.com/what/)
9. **Identity survives the account holder:** Apple's Legacy Contact documentation provides access to certain account data after death, with exclusions and access requirements. Transferable mechanism: the person ends, the permissions remain. Do not imply a trusted contact receives passwords or all purchases. [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102631)
10. **Inactivity becomes a trigger:** Google's Inactive Account Manager can notify people or share selected information after a configured period of inactivity. Transferable mechanism: a life reduced to an account status. It does not establish that all services keep charging after death. [Google Account Help](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/3036546?hl=en)
11. **Social systems need grief-aware design:** Meta's historical 2019 announcement discusses memorialized profiles, tributes and additional legacy-contact controls. Transferable mechanism: automatic social prompts can conflict with mortality. This is a historical product announcement, not verified current account-setting instructions. [Meta announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2019/04/updates-to-memorialization/amp/)
12. **Personality in funeral objects:** Creative Coffins offers personalisation and designs associated with interests, food and hobbies. Transferable mechanism: identity expressed through one recognizable mundane obsession, rather than generic skull decoration. Product prices and availability were not investigated. [Official product catalogue](https://www.creativecoffins.co.uk/our-coffins/)
13. **Names and dates carry the person:** London Museum explains personalized details on mourning jewellery. This supports a named, dated keepsake as a meaningful format; it does not establish that humour was those objects' purpose. [London Museum](https://www.londonmuseum.org.uk/blog/mourning-jewellery-love-loss/)

### Inferences

- Use one real time metric, then change conceptual category: unfinished business, account settings, personal objects, reputation, customer service.
- Let the apparently neutral left column set up the joke; deliver the contradiction in the right column.
- Make the joke about the reader's universal human predicament. Avoid mocking particular relatives, illnesses or recently bereaved people.
- A compact arc works better than unrelated gags: account opened → time spent → things left behind → final checkout → warm footer.
- Distinguish solemn legacy from satire. The name deserves visual prominence; the joke should not replace the identity of the person.

### Gaps

No audience testing or cross-cultural validation. No evidence that a particular line will improve sales. These sources cover representative formats, not all epitaphs, gadgets or death humour online. No future funeral count, number of graveyard visits or cause/date of death can be inferred from these sources.

Lines involving relatives or an executor should not be interpreted as instructions about estate law. Do not attach fabricated numerical predictions to these labels. The final warm line is deliberately less comic and works best near the footer.

## 3. Which ten are strongest, and how should they be used?

### Takeaway

Lead with short retail absurdity, move into ordinary life, finish with legacy and a nudge to act while alive. Keep one count of remaining weeks, not identical counts of Mondays and weekends.

### Cited Findings

The personal, practical emphasis is consistent with the everyday conversation frame of [Death Cafe](https://deathcafe.com/what/) and the personal identity preserved in [mourning jewellery](https://www.londonmuseum.org.uk/blog/mourning-jewellery-love-loss/). These are design inferences, not claims that those organisations endorse a commercial receipt.

### Inferences — strongest ten original drafts

1. **EXPIRY DATE — NOT ON THE LABEL:** immediately comprehensible; acknowledges uncertainty instead of pretending to predict a death date.
2. **EXTENDED WARRANTY — NICE TRY:** tightly connected to NO REFUNDS and to the existing playful warranty selector.
3. **LOYALTY POINTS — NON-TRANSFERABLE:** a recognisable receipt field whose value also makes an existential point.
4. **LAST WORDS — DRAFT UNSAVED:** a small modern irritation becomes final; compact enough to read instantly.
5. **IMPORTANT EMAILS — NO LONGER IMPORTANT:** changes emotional scale without gore or a numerical guess.
6. **PERSONAL BELONGINGS — NOW OTHER PEOPLE'S:** direct reversal of ownership; provides a different topic from time and technology.
7. **FUNERAL PLAYLIST — NO SKIP BUTTON:** an imagined funeral situation with a specific, everyday frustration; not a demographic prediction.
8. **LEGACY FORMAT — MOSTLY ANECDOTES:** warm and human, and sets up the later family/connection section.
9. **TIME SPENT WORRYING — NON-REFUNDABLE:** delivers the brand premise through a recognizable behaviour rather than another countdown.
10. **UNMADE PHONE CALLS — STILL POSSIBLE:** strongest closing beat; interrupts fatalism and gives the reader agency.

Suggested compact sequence for one edition: EXPIRY DATE; EXTENDED WARRANTY; LAST WORDS; IMPORTANT EMAILS; PERSONAL BELONGINGS; FUNERAL PLAYLIST; LEGACY FORMAT; UNMADE PHONE CALLS. Rotate the remaining choices in other editions; do not simply multiply the number of lines.

A possible short original story in receipt form: the warranty cannot be extended; an unfinished message becomes a legacy problem; the belongings change owner; the supposedly urgent inbox loses relevance; the stories are what remains. Then the footer: **THANKS FOR LIVING, COME AGAIN!** The comma is part of the requested copy.

### Gaps

Final typesetting needs print-size QA at 4 × 16 inches; character counts alone cannot establish legibility. No recommendation here authorizes publication, live checkout or changes to the Zazzle listing. The list is a writing palette, not a statistical model or forecast of anyone's life.
