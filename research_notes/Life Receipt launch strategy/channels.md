# Organic channels and 30-day launch sequence

## Which three acquisition channels deserve priority?

### Takeaway
Prioritize (1) short reveal videos on TikTok, (2) searchable Pinterest gift Pins, and (3) a few permission-compatible Reddit maker/design discussions. These are strategic hypotheses about Life Receipt, not proven channel performance. Allocate effort approximately 50% / 30% / 20%, with a capped 20-hour initial month and no spending; adjust after observed qualified visits and purchase intent.

### Cited Findings
- TikTok lists watching in full/skipping, sharing, commenting and liking among For You signals; interaction signals including watch time generally carry greater weight for most users. Its search system also considers query-content match. This supports testing an immediate visual reveal and explicit descriptive language, but does not establish a guaranteed recipe or reach. — [TikTok recommendations](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/exploring-videos/how-tiktok-recommends-content)
- TikTok's account-type help states personal accounts need at least 1,000 followers to put a website link on their profile. Its broad Business Account description says business accounts can link; the actual account and regional availability must be checked before assuming a clickable purchase path. Do not promise that merely switching account type unlocks it. — [TikTok account types](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/growing-your-audience/switching-to-a-creator-or-business-account)
- Pinterest permits image/video Pins with a title, description and destination link; its creation guidance includes related topics to aid discovery of similar ideas. Ordinary linked Pins provide a usable first experiment without assuming merchant approval. — [Create a Pin](https://help.pinterest.com/en/article/create-a-pin-from-an-image-or-video)
- Pinterest Product rich Pins require buyers to be able to purchase directly from the merchant site; eligibility and accurate product information also matter. The konsil.ch-to-Zazzle handoff therefore should not be assumed eligible for Product rich Pins or catalogs; ordinary Pins are the safer baseline. — [Rich Pins](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/rich-pins)
- Reddit prohibits repeated or unsolicited mass engagement and repetitive promotional content, recommends authentic participation, and says communities apply their own rules and moderator judgments. No universal right to promote follows from earning karma or following a supposed 10% rule. — [Reddit spam policy](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam)
- Product Hunt says featuring is editorial and not guaranteed, no day guarantees a top spot, and makers should ask people to try the product and give feedback rather than request upvotes. — [Product Hunt launch FAQ](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/17734646-8-launch-faq-and-myths)

### Inferences
- **1. TikTok, surprise-to-preview experiment:** make six 12–20-second vertical videos over two weeks, two takes each for three hooks: “Your life. Itemized.”; “What would your lifetime receipt say?”; “A gift for someone who has everything—except an itemized life.” Begin with readable close-up, reveal the full elongated format, show personalization, end “Preview your own at konsil.ch/norefunds.” Vary the hook while retaining a common demonstration, then repeat the best two with a new example. Proposed durations/counts are test design, not platform rules.
- Use Standard and Sunshine first for broad gift discovery; test Dark separately as tasteful mortality humor, and Body through playful non-graphic numbers. Do not assume provocative editions will receive identical recommendation distribution. Never describe the product as predicting an individual's actual death date or medical future.
- Screen recording can start without expenditure, but label renders/preview footage honestly. If a physical sample already exists, film it beside a hand or familiar object to establish scale. Do not buy a sample or simulate a print being produced on a thermal printer as proof of the actual shipped item.
- Organic amplification: respond to genuine viewer questions with a fresh demonstrative video; use gift-intent terms in spoken/on-screen copy. No giveaway mechanics, tagging requests, bought engagement or unsolicited DMs are needed. Show the memorable URL if a clickable profile link is unavailable; log that attribution will be weaker.
- **2. Pinterest, gift-intent experiment:** create eight genuinely distinct Pins, two each for Standard/Body/Dark/Sunshine; use a full-length composition plus a legible detail crop or gift-context composition. Name concrete intent (“Personalized birthday gift: a life in receipts”, “Memento mori gift for dark-humor lovers”, “A cheerful personalized keepsake”) and accurately state physical print/order through Zazzle. Link each to the preview flow with per-creative UTM parameters; do not promise a free downloadable file.
- Publish those eight Pins across two weeks to two or three relevant boards (personalized gifts, unusual birthday gifts, memento mori art), then four fresh follow-ups based on actual clicks/saves. Avoid near-identical bulk pinning. Board/topic hypotheses require validation; no search-volume claims were found or assumed.
- **3. Reddit, authentic critique experiment:** shortlist at most five communities by reading current rules and recent accepted maker posts; pick at most two whose rules actually permit this. Art/pixel-art, creative coding/data art, and unusual gifts are candidate interests, not verified approved subreddit placements. Share a native image and a concrete design/process question, explicitly disclose “I made this; physical orders run through Zazzle.” Include a link only where allowed. If promotion rules are unclear, request moderator permission only in a future authorized execution step. Do not evade a ban with a link in comments or profile directions.
- Reddit success is useful discussion and relevant preview traffic, not volume of submissions. Stay to answer technical/design questions; one specific process post can be valuable even with few clicks. Do not fabricate a customer discovery story or pose as a neutral shopper.
- Product Hunt is a secondary optional maker-feedback event after the three experiments, not one of the three priority channels: the generator is potentially launchable, but physical gift buyers are a less certain match than gift/design audiences. This audience-fit judgment is our hypothesis, not a platform prohibition. No necessity to spend a full launch day chasing a leaderboard.

### Gaps
- Existing account access, follower counts, business verification, audience, physical sample availability, and baseline traffic are unknown. None is assumed.
- TikTok profile linking has account/region complexity; official account-type copy is broad. Verify the actual profile field and working visitor-view link before using “link in bio.”
- Current individual subreddit rules and posting eligibility have not been checked; no specific subreddit is approved by this research.
- No validated conversion rate, reachable audience, viral probability, platform revenue, keyword demand or sales forecast is available.

## What is the executable 30-day sequence?

### Takeaway
Run a measured creative test, then concentrate on one winning message/channel. Starting 25 November 2026 makes Day 30 24 December; holiday fulfillment must be verified before presenting this as a Christmas-delivery offer.

### Cited Findings
- TikTok recommendation signals support examining viewing behavior as well as downstream action; Pinterest supports destination links; Reddit enforces community-specific expectations. These platform mechanics inform the experimental order, but not the proposed calendar or quantity of assets. — [TikTok recommendations](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/exploring-videos/how-tiktok-recommends-content); [Create a Pin](https://help.pinterest.com/en/article/create-a-pin-from-an-image-or-video); [Reddit spam policy](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam)

### Inferences
- **Days 1–3, Nov 25–27 (4 hours):** confirm the working mobile preview-to-Zazzle journey without ordering; record displayed destination-country price/shipping information; inventory account capabilities and existing sample. Choose one public name (“Life Receipt”, with “Lifetime Receipt” only as explanatory copy) to keep search/recall consistent. Create one base screen recording, eight visual layouts and hook scripts. Check measurement feasibility before publishing. This is proposed work, not work executed here.
- **Days 4–10, Nov 28–Dec 4 (5 hours):** publish three TikTok hook tests and four Pinterest Pins, subject to execution authorization; read community rules and prepare two original Reddit posts, posting at most one where permitted. Answer substantive comments. Capture metrics at the same elapsed age for each creative, e.g. 48 hours and seven days.
- **Days 11–17, Dec 5–11 (4 hours):** publish the other three videos and four Pins. Use one native Reddit process/critique post only if independently suitable and allowed, not a repeat of the first. Review: which edition/hook brings product understanding, qualified previews and Zazzle intent rather than empty likes?
- **Days 18–24, Dec 12–18 (4 hours):** create two new versions of the strongest video hook, four new Pins, and one comment-response explainer about personalization, print size, or shipping. Shift toward birthday/anniversary/self-gift positioning when Christmas arrival cannot be verified. Do not imply digital instant delivery; the preview is free to view, the print is purchased and shipped.
- **Days 25–30, Dec 19–24 (3 hours):** compare cohorts, attributable orders if available, and hours invested. Continue only the best channel/message, repair any obvious conversion uncertainty before adding distribution, and write a decision for the next month. Product Hunt can be reconsidered only if there is a clear maker-demo asset and time remains; it should not displace a channel already producing credible buyer intent.
- Minimum deliverable for the month: ten TikTok videos, twelve Pinterest Pins, and up to two context-specific Reddit submissions. These are workload caps/test samples, not a promise of reach. Reduce counts if the 20-hour cap is exhausted.

### Gaps
- Thanksgiving/Black Friday timing does not establish delivery eligibility or product-market demand. Zazzle fulfillment country/date information is outside this channel research and must be verified separately.
- This assignment authorizes research only. No account creation, posting, outreach, moderator messages, site editing or spending has occurred.

## How should acquisition economics and stop decisions be measured?

### Takeaway
Optimize verified contribution per creator-hour, with funnel actions as provisional signals while orders are sparse. Views and saves are useful diagnostics; they do not establish sales or profitability.

### Cited Findings
- Pinterest's linked Pins can take visitors to a destination; TikTok personal profile links have an explicit follower restriction. Attribution feasibility differs before any creative performance comparison. — [Create a Pin](https://help.pinterest.com/en/article/create-a-pin-from-an-image-or-video); [TikTok account types](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/growing-your-audience/switching-to-a-creator-or-business-account)

### Inferences
- Proposed tracking: source/medium/campaign/content UTMs for each usable link; record creator hours, video watch/completion metrics where accessible, shares/saves, tagged landing visits, preview starts/completions if existing analytics expose them, Zazzle outbound clicks, and confirmed attributable orders/royalties where records actually permit. Do not silently assume analytics integrations exist or add them during this research task.
- Keep Zazzle outbound clicks distinct from orders. Cross-domain attribution may end at handoff; if so report “purchase intent / outbound clicks” and reconcile actual royalty/order reporting separately. Never call a checkout click a sale.
- Economic accounting: contribution = creator royalty actually retained minus per-order creator costs and allocated refunds/adjustments. Commercial revenue is not necessarily retail selling price because Zazzle collects/manufactures/ships. Organic acquisition still has time cost: contribution per hour = attributable contribution divided by creator hours; fully loaded acquisition cost = time valued at the user's chosen hourly rate plus any authorized expenses, divided by confirmed attributable new orders.
- Use matched creative age and internal relative comparisons; there are no invented industry conversion benchmarks. After two distinct creative rounds, pause a channel with no qualified visits/meaningful intent and high hours; choose the channel producing the clearest verified buyer behavior per hour. With very small denominators, label results inconclusive rather than extrapolating.
- If videos get watch time/shares but no visits, investigate unavailable links, unclear CTA and weak gift positioning; if visitors preview but do not click Zazzle, investigate comprehension/value; if handoff clicks occur without confirmed orders, inspect visible all-in price, shipping and handoff trust before making more content. These are diagnostic hypotheses, not proved causal findings.

### Gaps
- Actual royalty/margin, refund adjustments, all-in delivered prices, attribution instrumentation and the user's hourly time value are not supplied. Cannot calculate allowable acquisition cost, break-even sales or profit projections yet.
