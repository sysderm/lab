# Life Receipt acquisition economics — checked 2026-11-25

## What can actually be earned and attributed?

### Takeaway
Treat the 5% creator royalty as a gross scenario, not a verified payout. No paid acquisition is justified until the exact direct-only template + generated customer artwork + Linkover route produces a paid, delivered, retained order with visible earnings and attribution. Artwork retention is not commission confirmation.

### Cited Findings
- Creator gross royalty uses the selected rate and Net Royalty Sales, excludes shipping, tax, refunds and special upcharges (upcharges have separate standard-rate treatment). Marketing fees can reduce gross royalty; rates above10% trigger a5% fee on gross royalty. Zazzle sets selling prices and adjusts them when royalties increase; currency conversion can have fees. — [Creator License Agreement §§3.2–3.8](https://www.zazzle.com/terms/creator_license_agreement)
- Published physical-product standard royalty is5%; Wall Art & Décor has40% total self-promotion earnings and15% cross-promotion referral commission, and a marketing fee of40% of gross royalty. These are department rules, not confirmation for this product. — [Official department table](https://www.zazzle.com/hc/article/ambassador_referral_commissions_creator_royalties-30810357489175)
- Self-promotion specifies Public Products containing the creator’s Primary Content; others’ uploaded/created content is excluded from self-promotion commission. Proper tracking is mandatory; tracking failure pays no commission. Referral validity is45 days, with replacement possible after14 days; eligible purchases must be on the linked domain, paid, delivered and unrefunded. Referral basis excludes shipping, taxes and reversals. — [Ambassador Agreement §§2–4](https://www.zazzle.com/terms/ambassadors_agreement)
- Royalty and referral are separately reported. For self-promotion the commission fills the earnings-goal difference; never add5% to40%. Links with rf plus Ambassador ID earn cross-promotion rather than self-promotion rates. Transaction Details clarify actual calculations. — [Creator FAQ, updated January5,2026](https://www.zazzle.com/hc/article/ambassador_program_faqs_creator-37460907460631)
- General FAQ describes free creator membership and manufacturing/fulfillment handled by Zazzle. It loosely says creators determine final prices, while the controlling license says Zazzle determines offered prices: use actual Zazzle royalty/material configuration and a refreshed quote, not an independently invented storefront price or formula. — [General FAQ](https://www.zazzle.com/hc/article/frequently_asked_questions-115000209913)
- Regular payout thresholds are$50 PayPal/$100 check; required payee/tax information and payout timing apply. Below-threshold payout requests can incur fees. Recorded earnings therefore differ from money received. — [Earnings payments](https://www.zazzle.com/hc/article/earnings_payments_to_zazzle_creators-219577928)

## What is the cheapest defensible measurement and launch sequence?

### Takeaway
Use existing aggregate counters and manual Zazzle reconciliation first. Authorize no purchases, subscriptions, outreach, campaigns or account mutations through this research. Recommended eventual distribution starts with free owned/organic channels only after attribution clarification and spike checks.

### Cited Findings
- Zazzle supplies unaudited referral reports and may vary their frequency/content; reliably tracked, completed sales govern payment. — [Ambassador Agreement §§3–4](https://www.zazzle.com/terms/ambassadors_agreement)
- Referral reports and Royalty History Transaction Details expose different earnings components; a missing referral row can have a calculation explanation. — [Creator FAQ](https://www.zazzle.com/hc/article/ambassador_program_faqs_creator-37460907460631)

### Inferences
1. No-cost preflight with fictional data: site arrival → generator completion → upload success/failure → outbound Zazzle click → correct personalized artwork/variant in destination/cart. Separate these events from purchase. Do not complete paid checkout or add a subscription. Leave present tracking parameters intact until support resolves exact route.
2. Aggregate daily counts by a short allowlisted campaign/source label; optional random ephemeral session token only if needed to deduplicate, with short retention. Record no names, birthdays, generated image URLs, full destination URLs, query strings, order addresses, raw referrers or artwork payloads. Strip query strings from application/error/access logging where configurable. Use generic campaign IDs on the site, not personal-data parameters; do not alter supported Zazzle links merely to add analytics. Browser pageviews alone cannot prove a sale.
3. Maintain a tiny local aggregate ledger: date, site arrivals, completions, successful uploads, outbound clicks, total retained paid orders, creator gross/net royalty, referral earnings, reversals, net settled payout; source known/unknown. Reconcile existing Zazzle Royalty History/Referral Reports/Transaction Details manually. Count finalized paid retained orders separately from pending orders and clicks; no webhook currently available in project evidence. Do not promise user-level cross-domain attribution from aggregate totals.
4. Let a genuine independently intended organic order provide eventual purchase evidence, or seek explicit owner authorization for a controlled purchase later. Do not induce or buy an order now. One order proves a path, not a stable conversion estimate. Match privacy-safe time/product/campaign evidence where possible, mark ambiguous channel credit unknown, and confirm paid/delivered/refund status and actual fee lines before estimating CAC.
5. After support eligibility answer and at least one properly attributed retained sale, calculate observed E from finalized account earnings and conversion using one clearly stated denominator. Collect more organic observations before considering spend. Eligible audiences and free owned content/SEO/community posts are potential channels, but outreach/publication is not authorized by this assignment. Prefer sharing a safe generic preview and site URL; no personal artwork URL sharing.
6. Spike preflight must precede a viral post: supplied production limits are global2uploads/minute burst4, reads60/minute burst60, upload cap1MiB; existing Worker Free/R2 usage billing has zero base but finite quotas and possible variable costs. These are project facts supplied by coordinator, not independently cloud-verified. Two sustained successful upload starts/minute permit only120/hour globally; a burst of100 requests can reject most attempts. Verify friendly429/retry behavior, preserve generated preview on failure, bound retries/backoff, test current storage expiry/cleanup, and inspect available billing/usage controls without purchasing or changing plans. Read limits also constrain image retrieval spikes. Do not describe infrastructure as unlimited/free at any volume.
7. Keep privacy-safe usage/cost alerts and a documented manual pause procedure; no launchd/cron/tmux stops, cloud changes or paid services are part of this research. Monitor uploads,429s,read failures,R2 storage/operations and Worker requests during any later authorized free-channel release.

### Gaps
No measured funnel, no retained attributed purchase, no verified usable net margin; aggregate manual reconciliation cannot perfectly attribute simultaneous channels. Cloud quota/billing ceilings, object lifecycle and whether per-minute limits are per endpoint need deployment-level verification by the main agent; this research does not invent provider guarantees.
